# Breast Thermography Systematic Review

Supplementary data from a systematic literature review on the application of deep learning techniques to breast thermographic image classification.

## Overview

The systematic review covers studies published between January 2020 and December 2025.

The review identified 390 records through searches in PubMed, Scopus, ScienceDirect, IEEE Xplore, and Wiley Online Library. Following duplicate removal and eligibility screening, 90 studies were included.

## Dataset

The Excel workbook `Planilha_Revisão_Sistemática.xlsx` contains information collected during the review, including:

- Bibliographic information about the studies.
- Breast thermography datasets.
- Image preprocessing techniques.

The extracted information supports the analysis of methodological trends, commonly used approaches, limitations, and research opportunities.

## Purpose

The dataset is provided to support transparency, reproducibility, and future research on deep learning applied to breast thermography.

## Notes

The spreadsheet contains information extracted from published scientific studies. The results reported in the original publications remain attributable to their respective authors.
